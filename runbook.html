5. Three questions you will probably get

"Is the compression real or simulated?" Real. Pruning removes weights with structured L2 pruning on convolutions and makes the masks permanent, INT8 uses torch's dynamic quantization, and distillation runs six epochs of Adam training against the teacher. The artifact sizes are measured after writing.

"How do you know the smaller model still works?" Both models run on the same sixteen seeded inputs and we compare the outputs, by predicted class for classifiers and with a small numeric tolerance otherwise. That is output agreement, which we label honestly as a behavioural check and not as test set accuracy.

"Why is the planner trustworthy?" It predicts with visible factors, filters anything the target hardware cannot run, scores with published weights, and keeps rejected plans with the reason they were rejected. Nothing is a black box, and every number after execution is measured.

The viva sheet has about fourteen more, with full answers.
